# Course assessment-preparation procedure

## Purpose

Coordinate assessment preparation from project-owned scope, contextual relevance
evidence, skill dependencies, and current mastery.

## Authority versus relevance

Course/source authority and assessment relevance are separate. Evaluate relevance
using evidence such as explicit current instructor guidance, demonstrated
problem-form correspondence, instructor specificity, recency, source role,
coverage gaps, and prerequisite/mastery dependencies.

Do not hardcode old exam, practice set, homework, lecture example, or review sheet
as globally superior.

## Procedure

1. Recover assessment scope and label uncertainty.
2. Collect only the relevance evidence needed for the current preparation
   decision and record why it matters.
3. Map scope to atomic skills, prerequisites, mastery, known traps, and stale
   evidence.
4. Prioritize blocking/high-relevance weaknesses and coverage gaps.
5. Route interactive learning through `procedures/course_study_session.md` and
   document production through `procedures/document_artifact_build.md` as
   appropriate.
6. Validate coverage, artifact completion, and mastery/readiness separately.

Time pressure may change tutoring mode or artifact scope but never authorizes
false mastery claims or fabricated source evidence.
